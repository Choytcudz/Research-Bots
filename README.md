# Research-Bots

Process reports from a documentary-corpus research project on how institutions operationalize the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act (IDEA) for dually involved youth with disabilities.

Author: Joseph Castellanos Hoyt. Reports were prepared with the assistance of research bots and are published as process documentation, not as findings. Nothing here labels any agency action a violation, and the absence of a document is treated as a lead only.

## Contents

- `reports/JJ_Utah/`: Utah juvenile justice track. A literature-supported, PRISMA-style process report (Markdown, Word and PDF), its source text, and the script that builds the Word file.

Each research track has its own folder under `reports/`.
